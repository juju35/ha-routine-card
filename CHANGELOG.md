# Changelog

## 1.0.0 - 2026-11-27

### Features
- First version: `routine-card` and `routine-mini-card`. A routine's steps as tiles (done with the time, in progress with a timer, to do with the duration), the step in progress with a progress ring and what comes next, a status pill (*3 / 6 · 9 min*, *Not started*, *All done*, *Late*), the week's stars with a goal and a reward, and a button to mark the step done.
- Illustrated header: a house on a hill, the morning sun rising with the progress, the evening windows going dark step by step, sparkles once done; day and night themes, 22 languages, visual editor.
